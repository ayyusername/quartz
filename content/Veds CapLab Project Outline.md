![[Pasted image 20240309120227.png]]

3 main ideas: 

--Aortic dissection/rupture are a/the leading cause of death in vEDS. 

--Excess PKC/ERK signaling environment, as well as normal oxytocin signaling during lactation and androgen signaling at puberty, increase the risk of aortic rupture. 

--Targeted inhibition of these signaling pathways **prevents** death due to aortic dissection.  



vEDS aortas display a molecular signature for excessive PKC/ERK signaling; inhibition of excessive PKCβ or ERK signaling prevents death due to aortic dissection.
